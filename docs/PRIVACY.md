# Privacy Policy – Fitness Coach

_Effective date: 2026-10-16_

Fitness Coach is a private, non-commercial application operated by one individual (the "operator") for personal use only. It is not offered to the public.

## What data is processed
When the operator connects their own WHOOP and Wahoo accounts, the application reads, via the official APIs and with the scopes granted during sign-in: profile, body measurements, recovery, sleep, physiological cycles and workouts (WHOOP), and user profile and workouts including workout files (Wahoo). The operator may also upload workout files (e.g. FIT) manually.

## How it is used
The data is used solely to show the operator their own training and health information, compute training metrics and generate training suggestions for the operator. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train machine-learning models.

## Where it is stored
Data and OAuth tokens are stored in the operator's own Cloudflare account (Cloudflare Workers and D1). Access is restricted to the operator. Requests made by the operator through Claude (Anthropic) to view or analyse their data are processed under the operator's own Claude account.

## Retention and deletion
Data is kept as long as the operator uses the application. Disconnecting a provider revokes the stored tokens; the operator can delete all stored data at any time. Access can also be revoked from the provider's own account settings.
